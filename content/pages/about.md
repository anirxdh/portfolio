Anirudh Vasudevan is a full-stack and AI engineer based in San Francisco. He is a Member of Technical Staff at [Rivo](https://www.rivofi.com/), a San Francisco fintech, where he builds production voice AI agents and owns the analytics and growth stack. There are other people with the same name, so if you are looking for the engineer behind [LARK](/blog/lark-multi-agent-mcp/), [Living Photos](/blog/living-photos-gaussian-splats/), or [ScreenSense](/blog/screensense-voice-browser-agent/), you are in the right place.

## What I do now

I am a Member of Technical Staff at Rivo. Most of my week goes into conversational AI that real customers talk to:

- **Voice and agentic AI.** I deploy conversational and agentic AI in Python on cloud microservices, including LiveKit voice agents that capture leads and an internal support chatbot.
- **One app, two platforms.** I own the iOS and web apps, which run on a single React Native codebase that I migrated from Next.js. Playwright and Maestro end-to-end tests and Sentry monitoring keep it honest.
- **Speed.** I added TanStack Query caching with endpoint-level invalidation, which cut homepage load time by 87 percent and improved p95 latency.
- **Growth.** I run full-funnel tracking and paid ads across TikTok, Meta, Reddit, and Google, built an admin-configurable referral system, and built a pipeline that generates UGC-style video ads end to end.

Before Rivo I was the founding engineer at Nonlinear, a San Francisco AI startup. There I built the Universal Node System for AI workflows, which cut configuration time by 45 percent, and agentic RAG pipelines behind context-aware agents and a Microsoft Teams bot, which cut retrieval latency by 60 percent.

Before that I was a graduate research assistant at the University of Minnesota's Institute for Health Informatics, doing data processing, causal analysis, and predictive modeling on K-12 education and health equity in Minnesota.
